Voice API Account Setup

Before connecting to various intelligent voice services, you first need to register an account on the voice platform and obtain a dedicated API key. This key serves as your "voice pass" to the intelligent voice service, handling crucial tasks such as identity verification, interface calls, and speech synthesis.
