Privacy Policy

Welcome to TAVO (hereinafter referred to as "the Application").This policy aims to clearly explain how we handle your personal information. Please read carefully before use. By continuing to use the Application, you are deemed to have agreed to all the contents of this policy.

I. How We Handle Your Information

1. Local Data Storage

The Application employs a fully localized storage strategy. The following data is stored solely on your device and will not be uploaded to any server:

Chat history

Personalized settings and preferences

API key information

User-created role data

Other application-related configurations

Your Rights: You may delete the above data at any time through device settings or in-app functions.

2. Data Transmission

When you use the AI chat features, the following data is transmitted directly from your device to the AI provider you have configured (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint you specify). Tavo does not operate a backend server that stores or relays this content.

What is transmitted:

Your messages

Conversation history (for context continuity)

Character and persona configuration

Lorebook entries you have created or imported

Presets and system prompts

Long-term memory content

Model parameters (e.g., temperature, max tokens)

Authentication: Your API key is used only to authenticate the request to the provider you selected. It is stored locally on your device and never sent to Tavo's servers.

Transport security: All requests use HTTPS encryption.

Third-party responsibility: Once data reaches the AI provider, it is governed by that provider's privacy policy and terms of service. We recommend reviewing them (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) before use.

II. Third-Party Services

Third-party services integrated into the Application (e.g., AI interfaces) are independently provided by external suppliers. Please note:

You must independently agree to and comply with the third-party terms of service and privacy policies.

We cannot control third-party data processing practices. We recommend evaluating their security cautiously.

If you do not wish your data to be transmitted to third parties, you may discontinue using related features.

III. Policy Updates and Notifications

1.We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Updates will be announced via in-app pop-ups or on the official website.

2.Significant changes (e.g., changes to data usage purposes) will be published on the official website or within the Application.

3.Continued use of the Application constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
