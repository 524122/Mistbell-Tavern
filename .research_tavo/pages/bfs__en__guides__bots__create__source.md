Source

“Character Origin & Attribution”

What is this for？

👉 Used to indicate the origin of the character, such as the creator’s ID or the original source link.

The original creator can edit or replace this information

Other users may add notes or references, but cannot override the creator’s source

Examples:

https://tavoai.dev/app/index.html#/

or

Tavo.ai @tavo

📌 Tips

Having a clear source makes the character more trustworthy

Especially important for re-sharing and derivative works

⚠️ Important Notice

We only ensure that this field cannot be edited or removed by other users within Tavo. However, the file may be altered or damaged outside of Tavo.

If you reinstall Tavo or switch to a different device, you may lose the ability to edit or delete this field.
