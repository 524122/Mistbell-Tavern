Local TTS Speech Service Configuration

Before using AI chat applications, you need to properly configure TTS voice services to make your AI characters "speak." TTS (Text-to-Speech) services can convert AI character responses into natural and fluent speech, making your conversations with virtual characters more immersive and realistic.

Through appropriate voice configuration, each AI character can have a unique voice that matches their personality traits, thereby enhancing the overall conversational experience and immersion.

This page provides you with configuration tutorials for TTS voice services suitable for AI chat applications, helping you get started quickly without searching through complicated documentation.
