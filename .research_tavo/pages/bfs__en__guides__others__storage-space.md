Storage Space

You can use this feature to view detailed information about the storage space used by the application and perform targeted data cleanup to free up device space.

Operation Path

Open the left sidebar.

Click the "More" option, find and select "Settings" in the menu.

After entering the settings interface, select the "Storage Space" tab.

Space Details and Cleanup

After entering, you will see the following information and options:

Tavo Used Space: Visually displays the total storage capacity currently occupied by the application.

Cleanable Data: Lists data categories that can be safely cleaned up and their occupied space, including:

◦ Cache: TTS voice cache. After cleanup, playback will re-call the API.
◦ Logs: Context logs, load balancing logs, etc.
◦ Characters: Stored character avatars, images, etc.

Please Note: To ensure the safety of your conversation records and core settings, the "Data" category (typically containing information such as chats, characters, and world books) does not provide a cleanup option.

You can select the corresponding category for cleanup to free up storage space.
