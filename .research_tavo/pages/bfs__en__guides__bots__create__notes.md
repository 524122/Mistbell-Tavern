Creator Notes

“Notes & Tips for Users”

What is this for?

👉 This section is written for people who download and use the character.

The character itself does not see this

It appears in the character list / info panel

What can you write here?

How the character is best enjoyed

Recommended models

Any special settings or mechanics to be aware of

Emotional tone, boundaries, or stylistic notes

Example:

A casual, romance-focused character best suited for slow, everyday conversations.

Tested on GPT-4 and Claude models.

For smaller models, enabling example dialogues is recommended.

📌 Tips

Write it like a note from the creator, not a rulebook

Especially useful for Tavo characters or shared/public characters
