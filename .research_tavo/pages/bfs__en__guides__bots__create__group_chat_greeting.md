Group Only Greetings

“Group Only Greetings Messages”

What is this for?

👉 The first line the character says when entering a group chat.

Only triggers in group chat mode

Does not affect one-on-one conversations

If multiple lines are provided, one will be randomly selected

Separate each line with<START>

When should you write this?

You want the character to feel present and natural in group chats

The character’s group-chat tone should differ from private conversations

You don’t want group chats to start with lines meant for one-on-one intimacy

Examples:

Hey—this place is busier than I expected. I’ll just hang out and see what’s going on 👀

<START>

So… what are we talking about? Feels like I missed a few chapters.

<START>

Wow, this group’s lively. Am I late to the party?

📌 Tips

Group chats work best with a light, casual, low-pressure tone

Avoid flirty or exclusive lines meant for a single user
