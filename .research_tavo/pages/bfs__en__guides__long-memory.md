Long Memory Documentation

🍃 What is  Long Memory?

Imagine having multiple conversations with an AI, and everything you discuss can be remembered and referenced in future conversations. This is Long Memory. It allows the AI to remember what you've said without forgetting after each conversation ends. Your preferences, interests, and important details are all preserved and can be recalled at any time.

🌟 Difference Between Enabling and Not Enabling Long Memory

With Long Memory Enabled:

a. The AI acts like a friend with good memory, remembering things you've mentioned before. Whether it's your favorite movies, preferred foods, or personal habits, it remembers everything and can reference them in future conversations.

b. For example, if you mention that you enjoy running, months later the AI will still remember and might even ask if you've been running lately.

Without Long Memory Enabled:

a. After each conversation ends, the AI forgets everything, starting fresh every time. It won't remember anything you've said, and when you bring up topics again, it starts from zero.

b. If you tell the AI you like cats, it will listen, but the next time you mention it, it will have completely forgotten.

🧠 What is Long Memory Management?

Long Memory Management is the process of controlling what information the AI saves, when it saves it, and when it updates. You can decide which information is particularly important and which can be ignored. Simply put, it's how you control what the AI remembers and when it remembers it.

📝 What is "Manual Memory Extraction and Automatic Extraction After 10 Messages"?

Manual Memory Extraction:

a. You can actively tell the AI at any time: "This conversation is important, please remember it." It's like putting your conversation with the AI into a memory box that can be accessed anytime.

b. For example, if you and the AI discuss your favorite book and you think it's important, you can click to extract and have the AI remember it.

Automatic Extraction After 10 Messages:

a. The system automatically selects and saves valuable content after you and the AI have exchanged 10 messages. You don't need to intervene; the system does this automatically.

b. For instance, if you chat about your interests, the system will consider this meaningful content and automatically record it.

🔄 Difference Between Manual Extraction and Automatic Extraction After 10 Messages

Manual Memory Extraction:

You have active control over when to save memories, suitable for preserving particularly important content or things you really want to remember.

It's like selecting special photos and putting them in an album yourself.

Automatic Extraction After 10 Messages:

The system automatically makes decisions after you and the AI have conversed for 10 rounds, saving meaningful content.

For example, when you're having a great chat with the AI, the system will automatically "select" things you mention repeatedly and save them for you.
