Character Voice Binding

You can bind exclusive voices to characters to achieve a personalized voice conversation experience.

Operation Path

Open the left sidebar.

At the bottom, click the "More" option.

From the expanded menu, select "Voice".

After entering the voice settings interface, select "Character Voice Binding".

In the character list, locate the character that needs voice binding.

Click the voice binding icon on the right side of the character, then select and bind your preferred voice from the available voice library.

Note ⚠️

TTS Custom OpenAI Protocol Model：Character Voice Binding -> Select Character -> Manually Enter Voice ID -> Complete Binding
