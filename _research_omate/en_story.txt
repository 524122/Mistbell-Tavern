#
Stories
OMate&#x27;s story mode provides an immersive narrative experience, allowing you to read and experience interactive stories created by AI.
#
Story List
The story list page displays all your stories, with each story card containing the following information:
Story title
Author information
Story introduction
Participating character avatars
#
Importing Stories
You can import stories through the following methods:
On the story list page, click the import button in the top right corner
Enter the story link or upload a story file
After confirming the import, the story will be added to your list
Some characters on the discovery page also come with storybooks, which can be imported from the character card details page
Tip
When the network is slow, the import operation may take a longer time. Please be patient.
Tip
Imported stories are saved locally, and you can read and manage them at any time.
#
Reading Stories
#
Start Reading
Click on any story in the story list
Enter the story details page
Click "Continue Reading" to begin the experience
#
Reading Interface
The story reading interface contains the following elements:
Story title
Dialogue content display area
Continue reading button
Story information button
#
Reading Progress
The system automatically saves your reading progress
You can exit at any time and continue from where you left off next time
After finishing the story, you can choose to start reading again
#
Story Information
Each story has a detailed information page where you can view:
Story name
Author information
Story introduction
List of participating characters
#
Deleting Stories
If you want to delete a story:
In the story list, swipe left on the story card
Click the "Delete" button
Confirm the delete operation
The delete operation cannot be undone, please proceed with caution.
