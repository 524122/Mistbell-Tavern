Application Introduction
OMate is an AI-based chat application designed to provide users with a personalized chat experience. You can:
&#x1F3AD;
Create or import character cards, what works in the Tavern works in OMate too
&#x1F511;
Use your API, including private and unlimited local models
&#x1F4D6;
Explore story mode to delve into the stories behind characters
&#x1F3AD;
Mask function, switch identities and interact deeply with AI
&#x1F9E0;
Long-term memory, allowing characters to remember every important moment with you
&#x1F4D9;
Customize built-in prompts to tell stories as you wish
